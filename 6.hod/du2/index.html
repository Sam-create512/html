about us

Our Vision

At Ararat Architecture, we transform spaces into inspiring environments. We believe architecture is more than just constructing buildings—it’s about shaping experiences, leaving lasting impressions, and enhancing the way people interact with their surroundings.

Our Approach

We blend creativity with precision to deliver designs that are both innovative and functional. From residential homes to commercial developments, every project is crafted with meticulous attention to detail, sustainability, and harmony with its environment.

Our Team

Our team of skilled architects and designers works closely with clients to understand their vision and bring it to life. Collaboration, creativity, and clear communication are at the heart of everything we do.

Why Choose Us

At Ararat Architecture, your vision becomes our blueprint for excellence. We are committed to creating designs that are not only beautiful but practical, sustainable, and timeless.

projects

Our Projects

At Ararat Architecture, every project tells a story. We take pride in designing spaces that are both functional and visually striking, reflecting the unique needs and vision of our clients.

Residential Designs

From modern homes to timeless family residences, we create living spaces that balance comfort, style, and practicality. Each home is tailored to the lifestyle of its inhabitants, blending innovation with enduring design.

Commercial & Public Spaces

Our commercial projects are designed to inspire and perform. We craft office buildings, retail spaces, and public facilities that enhance usability, foster collaboration, and leave a lasting impression.

Sustainable Architecture

Sustainability is at the core of our work. We integrate eco-friendly materials and energy-efficient solutions to create buildings that respect the environment while meeting modern needs.

Featured Work

Each project is a reflection of our dedication to design excellence. We focus on clean lines, innovative solutions, and timeless aesthetics that stand the test of time.

services

Our Services

At Ararat Architecture, we offer a full range of architectural services, guiding your project from concept to completion with creativity, precision, and care.

Architectural Design

We create innovative, functional, and aesthetically striking designs for residential, commercial, and public spaces. Every design is tailored to your vision, lifestyle, and needs.

Interior Design

Our interior design services ensure that every space is as beautiful inside as it is outside. We focus on harmony, functionality, and personalized style to create environments you’ll love to live or work in.

Project Management

From planning to execution, we oversee every stage of construction to ensure timelines, budgets, and quality standards are met. Our team coordinates seamlessly with contractors and stakeholders for a smooth process.

Renovation & Restoration

We bring new life to existing buildings while preserving their character and integrity. Our renovation and restoration services combine modern solutions with respect for history and context.

Sustainable & Green Design

Sustainability is at the heart of our approach. We integrate energy-efficient solutions, eco-friendly materials, and smart design strategies to minimize environmental impact while maximizing comfort and performance.
